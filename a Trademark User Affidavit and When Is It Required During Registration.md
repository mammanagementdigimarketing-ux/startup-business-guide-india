# What Is a Trademark User Affidavit and When Is It Required During Registration?

Trademark registration is an important step for businesses that want to protect their brand identity in India. When applying for trademark registration, applicants must provide accurate information about how and when they started using their brand name, logo or other trademark.

For businesses that have already been using a trademark before submitting their application, a **Trademark User Affidavit** plays an important role in establishing the claimed date of use. It provides supporting information about the trademark's commercial use and helps the Trademark Registry assess the applicant's claim.

## What Is a Trademark User Affidavit?

A Trademark User Affidavit is a sworn statement submitted by a trademark applicant to support a claim that a particular trademark has been used commercially before the trademark application date.

It generally explains:

- The date or period when the trademark was first used.
- The person or business that started using the trademark.
- The goods or services associated with the trademark.
- How the trademark has been used in business activities.
- Supporting documents that establish the claimed use.

Under Rule 25(2) of the Trade Marks Rules, 2017, applicants claiming trademark use before the application date must submit an affidavit confirming such use along with supporting documents.

## When Is a Trademark User Affidavit Required?

A Trademark User Affidavit is required when an applicant wants to claim that their trademark was already in use before filing the registration application.

For example, consider a business that has been selling products under a particular brand name since 2022 but decides to apply for trademark registration in 2026.

If the applicant wants to claim 2022 as the first-use date, an affidavit and supporting evidence must be submitted to establish that claim.

However, applicants filing their trademark applications under the "proposed to be used" category do not need to submit a prior-use affidavit.

## What Documents Can Support a Trademark User Affidavit?

Applicants should collect relevant documents that demonstrate the actual use of their trademark in connection with the claimed goods or services.

Common supporting documents include:

1. **Sales invoices:** Dated invoices showing products or services sold under the trademark.
2. **Marketing materials:** Advertisements, promotional brochures and campaign records.
3. **Product packaging:** Labels, packaging photographs and product branding.
4. **Business website:** Dated website records showing the trademark and associated offerings.
5. **Purchase orders:** Business transaction records containing the trademark.
6. **Commercial records:** Relevant business documents establishing the period of trademark use.

The evidence should be genuine, relevant and consistent with the claimed first-use date.

## What Information Should a Trademark User Affidavit Contain?

A properly prepared affidavit generally includes the following information:

- Applicant's full name and address.
- Trademark name, logo or representation.
- Trademark application details, where available.
- Description of relevant goods or services.
- Claimed date of first use.
- Details of the business activities involving the trademark.
- Explanation of how the trademark has been used.
- List of supporting documents attached as exhibits.
- Signature and verification by the person making the affidavit.

The affidavit should contain accurate information supported by available business records.

## How to Submit a Trademark User Affidavit in India

Businesses can follow these general steps when preparing their prior-use evidence:

**Step 1: Identify the First-Use Date**

Determine when the trademark was first commercially used in connection with the relevant goods or services.

**Step 2: Collect Supporting Evidence**

Gather invoices, advertisements, product photographs, website records and other relevant documents.

**Step 3: Prepare the Affidavit**

Draft an affidavit explaining the trademark's history, ownership and commercial use.

**Step 4: Verify the Information**

Ensure that the claimed first-use date matches the available supporting records.

**Step 5: File the Trademark Application**

Submit the trademark application through Form TM-A and include the affidavit and supporting documents when claiming prior use.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Businesses should be careful when preparing trademark user affidavits. Some common mistakes include:

- Claiming an incorrect first-use date.
- Providing insufficient supporting evidence.
- Submitting inconsistent business records.
- Using documents that do not establish the claimed trademark use.
- Providing incomplete information about goods or services.
- Confusing the proposed-to-be-used category with the prior-use category.

Accurate documentation can help reduce avoidable questions about the claimed use of a trademark.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Is a Trademark User Affidavit mandatory for every trademark application?

No. It is required when the applicant claims that the trademark was used before the application date. Applications filed on a proposed-to-be-used basis do not require a prior-use affidavit.

### 2. Can a startup apply for trademark registration without a user affidavit?

Yes. A startup can apply under the proposed-to-be-used category if it has not started using the trademark commercially.

### 3. What happens if the claimed first-use date is incorrect?

An incorrect first-use claim may lead to examination queries, requests for additional evidence or other complications. Applicants should provide accurate information supported by genuine records.

### 4. Can invoices be used as evidence of trademark use?

Yes. Invoices can support a prior-use claim when they clearly establish the relevant trademark, commercial activity and date.

### 5. Which rule governs the Trademark User Affidavit in India?

Rule 25(2) of the Trade Marks Rules, 2017, requires an affidavit and supporting documents when an applicant claims prior use of a trademark.

## Final Thoughts

A Trademark User Affidavit is an important supporting document for businesses claiming that their brand was used before filing a trademark application in India. It helps establish the claimed first-use date through a sworn statement and relevant commercial evidence.

Business owners should maintain accurate records and ensure that their affidavit reflects genuine trademark usage. Proper preparation can make the trademark registration process more organised and help protect valuable brand identity.
