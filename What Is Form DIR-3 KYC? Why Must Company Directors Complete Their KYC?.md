# What Is Form DIR-3 KYC? Why Must Company Directors Complete Their KYC?

Form DIR-3 KYC is a compliance requirement introduced by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) to maintain accurate and updated information about individuals holding a Director Identification Number (DIN) in India.

It helps the government verify directors' identities and maintain reliable records of individuals associated with registered companies.

For company directors, understanding DIR-3 KYC is important because keeping personal information updated is part of maintaining proper corporate compliance.

## What Is Form DIR-3 KYC?

DIR-3 KYC is a form used to verify and update the personal details of individuals who hold a Director Identification Number (DIN).

A DIN is a unique identification number allotted by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs to an individual who intends to become a director of a company.

Through this KYC process, directors provide or confirm important information, including:

- Full name and date of birth
- Permanent and residential address
- Personal mobile number
- Personal email address
- Identity and address verification details

The information helps the MCA maintain an accurate database of company directors.

## Why Is DIR-3 KYC Important for Company Directors?

Director KYC is important for maintaining accurate regulatory records and ensuring that directors fulfil their compliance responsibilities.

Here are some key reasons:

### 1. Maintains Updated Director Information

The KYC process ensures that the MCA has current information about directors. Updated contact details make official communication and regulatory notifications more reliable.

### 2. Helps Maintain DIN Status

Completing the applicable KYC requirement helps directors maintain their DIN status. Failure to comply with the prescribed requirements can result in DIN deactivation.

### 3. Supports Corporate Transparency

Verified director information helps improve transparency in company records and supports regulatory monitoring.

### 4. Helps Avoid Compliance Issues

Keeping KYC information updated reduces the possibility of missing important MCA communications and facing avoidable compliance complications.

## Who Is Required to Complete DIR-3 KYC?

Individuals holding a Director Identification Number are covered by the director KYC requirements under Rule 12A of the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Rules, 2014.

The requirement is not limited to directors of private limited companies. It also applies to relevant DIN holders associated with public limited companies and other companies covered by the rules.

Even if a director is not currently managing the company's daily operations, the applicable DIN-related KYC requirements must still be considered.

## What Is the DIR-3 KYC Due Date?

The DIR-3 KYC compliance framework has undergone an important change in 2026.

Previously, directors were generally required to complete their KYC every year by 30 September.

However, under the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Amendment Rules, 2025, effective from 31 March 2026, the annual filing requirement has been replaced with a three-year compliance cycle.

Under the revised Rule 12A:

- Directors must submit Form DIR-3 KYC Web once every third consecutive financial year.
- The filing must be completed by 30 June of the immediately following financial year.
- Changes to personal mobile numbers, email addresses or residential addresses must be reported within 30 days of the change.

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs has also clarified that directors who had completed their KYC before the revised provisions came into effect will generally have their next KYC filing due by 30 June 2028.

Therefore, directors should follow the applicable filing cycle rather than assume that the previous annual September deadline still applies.

## What Documents and Information Are Required?

Directors should keep the following information ready when completing their KYC:

- Director Identification Number (DIN)
- PAN card details
- Aadhaar or other applicable identity proof
- Residential address proof
- Personal mobile number
- Personal email address
- Digital Signature Certificate (DSC), wherever required

The exact documentation and verification requirements depend on the type of filing and the information being updated.

## What Happens If Directors Fail to Complete DIR-3 KYC?

Non-compliance with the applicable KYC requirements can create difficulties for directors.

Some possible consequences include:

- Deactivation of the Director Identification Number (DIN).
- Difficulties in completing certain MCA-related filings.
- Additional fees for reactivation, wherever applicable.
- Delays in completing corporate compliance procedures.

Under the earlier filing framework, a fee of ₹5,000 applied to certain delayed DIR-3 KYC filings. Directors should check the applicable fee provisions under the revised rules before submitting a delayed filing or seeking DIN reactivation.

## How Can Businesses Manage Director KYC Compliance?

Businesses can manage director KYC compliance more effectively by following a few practical steps:

1. Maintain updated records of all directors and their DINs.
2. Keep personal contact information accurate.
3. Track the applicable three-year KYC filing cycle.
4. Report changes in personal details within the prescribed 30-day period.
5. Verify the latest MCA requirements before filing.
6. Maintain acknowledgements and filing records for future reference.

Professional compliance assistance can also help businesses understand their filing responsibilities and avoid unnecessary procedural mistakes.
