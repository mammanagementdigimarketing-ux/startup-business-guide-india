# What Is Trademark Renewal in India? Process, Timeline and Documents

A trademark is an important business asset that protects a brand's name, logo, symbol or identity from unauthorised use. However, trademark registration in India is not permanent. It must be renewed periodically to maintain legal protection.

Under the Trade Marks Act, 1999, a registered trademark is valid for **10 years** and can be renewed for additional 10-year periods.

## 1. What Is Trademark Renewal?

Trademark renewal is the process of extending the validity of an existing trademark registration by submitting the prescribed application and government fee to the Indian Trademark Registry.

Businesses should renew their trademarks on time to maintain their registered rights and avoid the risk of removal from the register.

## 2. Trademark Renewal Timeline in India

Trademark owners can apply for renewal within the permitted period before the registration expires.

- **Validity:** 10 years.
- **Early renewal:** Application can be filed up to one year before expiry.
- **After expiry:** Renewal with the prescribed surcharge is generally possible within six months.
- **Restoration:** If removed for non-renewal, restoration may be requested within one year from expiry, subject to applicable requirements and the Registrar's decision.

## 3. Process for Trademark Renewal

The renewal process generally involves these steps:

1. **Check trademark status:** Verify the registration number, owner details and expiry date.
2. **Prepare documents:** Collect the registration certificate and relevant applicant details.
3. **File Form TM-R:** Submit the renewal application through the official IP India portal.
4. **Pay the government fee:** Pay the prescribed fee for each class.
5. **Track the application:** Monitor the application status and respond to any Registry communication.
6. **Renewal confirmation:** Check the updated registration details after approval.

## 4. Documents Required for Trademark Renewal

Commonly required information and documents include:

- Trademark registration certificate.
- Trademark registration number.
- Applicant or registered proprietor details.
- Identity and address details, where required.
- Authorisation or Power of Attorney, if filing through an authorised agent.
- Details of the relevant trademark class.
- Payment information for the prescribed fee.

The exact supporting documents may depend on the applicant and filing circumstances.

## 5. Trademark Renewal Fees

The official e-filing renewal fee listed by IP India is **₹9,000 per class**. Additional surcharge may apply for late renewal or restoration. Always verify the current fee schedule before filing.

## 6. Why Is Timely Trademark Renewal Important?

Renewing a trademark on time helps businesses:

- Maintain registered trademark protection.
- Protect brand identity and recognition.
- Reduce the risk of removal from the register.
- Support brand ownership and licensing activities.
- Maintain continuity of trademark registration.
