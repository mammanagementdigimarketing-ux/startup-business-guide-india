# Can a Private Limited Company Have Foreign Directors in India? Eligibility and Requirements

A Private Limited Company in India can appoint foreign nationals as directors, even if they are not Indian citizens. This allows overseas entrepreneurs, international investors and global businesses to participate in the management of Indian companies.

Under the Companies Act, 2013, foreign nationals can become directors if they meet the applicable eligibility and compliance requirements.

## Eligibility for Foreign Directors in India

Foreign nationals can become directors of Indian private limited companies, subject to the following conditions:

- They must be individuals.
- They must obtain a Director Identification Number (DIN).
- They must provide consent to act as a director through Form DIR-2.
- They must not be disqualified under Section 164 of the Companies Act, 2013.
- They must comply with applicable documentation and verification requirements.

## Minimum Director and Resident Director Requirements

A Private Limited Company must have at least two directors under Section 149 of the Companies Act, 2013.

Additionally, every company must have at least one resident director who has stayed in India for at least 182 days during the financial year. This requirement applies proportionately to newly incorporated companies.

A resident director does not necessarily have to be an Indian citizen. A foreign national can also fulfil this requirement if the prescribed residency condition is satisfied.

## Documents Required for Foreign Directors

Commonly required documents include:

- Valid passport
- Recent passport-size photograph
- Overseas residential address proof
- Email address and mobile number
- Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)
- Director Identification Number (DIN)
- Form DIR-2 consent

Documents issued outside India may require notarisation, apostille or consular authentication, depending on applicable requirements.

## Process of Appointing a Foreign Director

The appointment process generally involves:

1. Obtaining a Digital Signature Certificate.
2. Applying for DIN through the applicable MCA procedure.
3. Collecting consent through Form DIR-2.
4. Passing the required board resolution.
5. Filing Form DIR-12 with the Registrar of Companies within 30 days of appointment.
6. Completing applicable director KYC and statutory compliance.

Additional security clearance may be required for nationals of countries sharing a land border with India.

## Can Foreign Directors Hold Shares?

Yes. Foreign nationals can become directors and shareholders of an Indian private limited company. However, share investments must comply with applicable FEMA and Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) regulations.


## Final Thoughts

Foreign nationals can legally become directors of Indian private limited companies, provided they satisfy the requirements under the Companies Act, 2013. Businesses should carefully manage DIN applications, documentation, resident director requirements and MCA filings.
